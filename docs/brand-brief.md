# zaz   Brand brief

Hand this document to a designer or model when creating the **favicon**, **logo**, **app icon**, and related marks.

---

## 1. Who we are

**zaz** is a boutique software studio run by Alejandro Gómez Orozco. We turn ideas into working software   web apps, landing pages, automations, personalized tools, and AI agents   built to the client’s budget, with a money-back guarantee.

We are **not** a generic agency and **not** a product SaaS with seats and tiers. We sell **bespoke outcomes**: custom software that simplifies how someone works.

---

## 2. Brand priorities (non-negotiable)

Lead with these three ideas in every mark and lockup:

1. **The name: zaz**  
   Short, sharp, memorable. The wordmark is the hero   not a decorative symbol that outshines the letters. Prefer lowercase **zaz** in product UI and marketing.

2. **SaaS sensibility**  
   Feel like modern software: clean, product-native, digital-first. Think precision tooling and calm interfaces   not craft logos, mascots, or “startup rocket” clichés.

3. **Speed + simplicity**  
   We work fast and we simplify processes. The brand should feel **quick, light, and clear**   chaos reduced to order. Avoid busy marks, gradients overload, or anything that reads slow or bureaucratic.

---

## 3. Positioning one-liners

Use these as creative north stars (EN / ES as needed):

- **EN:** Your ideas into software.
- **EN:** Save time. Automate work. Stay in budget.
- **ES:** Tus ideas en software.

Promise: custom build, clear scope, money-back if it isn’t what was agreed. Risk on us.

## 4. Visual system (current product)

| Token | Light | Dark |
|-------|-------|------|
| Paper / background | `#FFFFFF` / `#F6F6F4` | `#0A0A0A` / `#111113` |
| Ink / text | `#0A0A0A` | `#F5F5F6` |
| Muted text | `#52525B` / `#8A8A93` | `#A1A1AA` / `#6B6B72` |
| Line / border | black @ 10% | white @ 12% |

- **Palette:** Monochrome only   white, off-white, gray, black. **No brand color accent** (no purple, no terracotta, no neon).
- **Typography:** Geist Sans (UI) + Geist Mono (rare technical labels). Expressive but restrained; logo may use a custom geometric sans related to this feel.
- **Motion metaphor:** Entropy → order (chaos settling into structure). Optional for marks that animate; static logo should still feel “settled,” not chaotic.
- **UI personality:** Light-first; dark mode supported. Soft glows and starfield CTAs exist on the site   marks should still work in flat black/white.

## 5. Logo & favicon guidance

### Wordmark
- Primary lockup: **zaz** (lowercase).
- Weight: medium–semibold; slightly tight tracking is fine if it stays legible at small sizes.
- Should work in solid black on white and solid white on black.
- Do **not** let an icon overpower the word. If an icon exists, it sits beside or integrates into the **z**, not above a tiny “zaz”.

### Symbol / mark (optional)
If a symbol is needed for favicon / app icon:
- Abstract, geometric, software-like.
- Themes that fit: **order from noise**, **path shortened**, **z as a bolt of clarity**   not literal clocks, robots, or checkmarks.
- Must read at **16×16** and **32×32**.
- Single-color silhouette preferred.

### Favicon / app icon
- Prioritize recognition of **zaz** or a strong single-glyph derived from **z**.
- Rounded-square app icon: monochrome or black field with white mark (or inverse).
- Avoid thin hairlines that vanish at favicon size.
- No gradients required; flat is preferred for clarity.

### Don’ts
- No purple / indigo AI clichés.
- No playful mascots or emoji.
- No all-caps “ZAZ” as the only lockup (screams sportswear / aggressive tech).
- No ornate serifs or script.
- No “fast” metaphors that are literal (speed lines, stopwatches) unless extremely abstract and still monochrome.

---

## 6. Tone of voice (for any lockup taglines)

- Direct, confident, short.
- Human founder energy   not corporate.
- Bilingual (EN / ES) market; marks themselves stay language-agnostic (**zaz**).

---

## 7. Deliverables to produce

Please output:

1. **Wordmark**   SVG (black) + SVG (white)
2. **Optional symbol**   SVG, monochrome
3. **Favicon set**   `favicon.ico`, `icon.svg` or PNG 32 / 180 (apple-touch), and a simple `site.webmanifest` icon if useful
4. **App / social avatar**   512×512 and 1024×1024 PNG, rounded-safe
5. **Clear space & minimum size**   one-pager note

File naming suggestion: `zaz-wordmark-black.svg`, `zaz-mark.svg`, `favicon.ico`, etc.

---

## 8. One-sentence creative brief

**zaz** is a monochrome, SaaS-sharp studio mark: the name first, speed and simplicity implied, chaos refined into software   never loud, never colorful, always clear at favicon size.
